# PaperSnap — Open-Access PDF & Citation Exporter

PaperSnap is a lightweight browser extension for students and researchers that locates legal open-access research PDFs and exports clean APA, BibTeX, and RIS citations in one click—with zero account login or browser lag.

## Features
- **1-Click Open-Access PDFs:** Automatically finds free full-text PDFs via Unpaywall and OpenAlex.
- **Universal Citation Export:** Copy APA or BibTeX citations, or download `.ris` files directly for EndNote Desktop, Zotero, and Mendeley.
- **PaperSnap Pro (One-Time License):** Includes Smart PDF Auto-Renaming (`Author_Year_Title.pdf`), unlimited reading list batch exports, and private on-device AI paper summaries.
